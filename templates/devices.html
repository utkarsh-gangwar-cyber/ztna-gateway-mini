About this simulation

A real ZTNA product receives device health from an endpoint agent. Here you set the four controls yourself. The score is the sum of the enabled controls (OS patched 30, antivirus 25, disk encryption 25, firewall 20). Try lowering a device's score, then request a protected resource with it: the next request is denied for low device trust.
